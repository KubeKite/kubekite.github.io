Industries

Where AI is reshaping the work

AI is not landing evenly. These are the domains growing fastest around it — what is being built in each, what the hard problems are, and what an engineer moving into one should understand.

01 — Healthcare & Life Sciences

From diagnosis to drug discovery

The most regulated place AI is being deployed, and the one with the least tolerance for a wrong answer.

  • Medical imaging
  • Drug discovery
  • Clinical workflow
  • Patient data privacy
02 — Financial Services

Decisions that have to be explainable

Where model output turns into money moved, and every call has to survive an audit.

  • Fraud detection
  • Underwriting & risk
  • Document processing
  • Model governance
03 — Legal & Compliance

Language work at scale

A field built entirely on documents, now the most natural fit for language models — and the most exposed.

  • Contract review
  • Discovery & research
  • Regulatory automation
  • Hallucination risk
04 — Robotics & Autonomy

When models meet the physical world

Physical AI, where latency, safety and the gap between simulation and reality decide everything.

  • Embodied agents
  • Self-driving
  • Warehouse automation
  • Sim-to-real
05 — Energy & Climate

Optimising systems too big to guess at

Grids, materials and climate models — plus the growing question of what AI itself consumes.

  • Grid optimisation
  • Materials discovery
  • Climate modelling
  • Datacentre demand
06 — Defence & Geospatial

Sensing, mapping and autonomy

Satellite and sensor data at a scale only machines can read, under unusually hard constraints.

  • Geospatial intelligence
  • Sensor fusion
  • Autonomous systems
  • Edge deployment
